About This Project

This project is an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze customer performance and product profitability using the Global Superstore dataset. The dashboards focus on KPIs, segmentation analysis, and time-based trends to convert raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

Key Insights

  • Sales grow steadily over time, while profit growth remains more moderate, indicating margin pressure.
  • Revenue is concentrated among a small group of customers and products, creating both opportunity and risk.
  • Consumer customers drive volume, while Corporate and Home Office segments show higher profit efficiency.
  • Technology products are the strongest revenue drivers but exhibit margin pressure.
  • Several products consistently generate losses, highlighting pricing and cost optimization opportunities.

Business Recommendations

  • Optimize pricing and discount strategies to improve margins without slowing growth.
  • Focus retention and upselling initiatives on high-value customer segments.
  • Reprice, bundle, or discontinue consistently loss-making products.
  • Invest in high-performing categories while improving cost efficiency elsewhere.
  • Use time-based trends to enhance demand forecasting and inventory planning.
