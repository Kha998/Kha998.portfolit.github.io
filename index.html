Medical Facilities Data Visualization Dashboard

An interactive data visualization dashboard designed to analyze medical facilities performance across cities and specializations. The project transforms raw healthcare data into actionable insights by visualizing doctor experience, patient satisfaction, wait times, consultation fees, and review trends. Built using D3.js, the dashboard enables dynamic filtering and rich interactivity, allowing users to explore patterns across medical specializations and locations. Key insights include identifying high-performing specializations, understanding experience distribution among doctors, and uncovering relationships between patient satisfaction, wait time, and consultation fees.
