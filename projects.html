Retail Banking Performance & Risk Analytics Dashboard


A complete data analysis project focuses on developing a Retail Banking Performance Analytics Dashboard using Power BI. This project built a Power BI dashboard integrating customer, transaction, and loan data to provide insights into customer segmentation, credit risk, fraud detection, and overall business performance. This project showcases data cleaning, transformation, data modelling and visualization skills across multiple tools to extract valuable business insights customer segmentation analysis credit risk monitoring fraud detection overall business performance tracking

The analysis is delivered through four integrated Power BI dashboards:
  • overall business performance tracking
    Provides a high-level summary of total customers, transactions, transaction amount, total loans, fraud transactions, and fraud rate.
  • customer segmentation analysis
    Analyzes customer demographics, deposit behavior, job distribution, age groups, and marital status to identify key customer segments.
  • Loan & Credit Risk Dashboard
    Monitors loan amount, default rate, credit score groups, and loan purpose to identify higher-risk borrowers.
  • Fraud Detection Dashboard
    Examines fraud transaction patterns by category, age group, velocity, and foreign transaction behavior.
  • Tools: Power BI
  • Techniques: DAX, Power Query, Data Modeling, Time-Series Analysis, Business Intelligence

HR Analytics Dashboard: Attrition, Demographics, Salary & Performance


This project presents an interactive HR analytics dashboard built using Power BI to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, and salary–performance alignment. The goal is to support data-driven HR decision-making by identifying retention risks, understanding workforce composition, and evaluating compensation effectiveness.

The analysis is delivered through three integrated Power BI dashboards, supported by insight and recommendation pages:
  • Attrition Analysis Dashboard
    Analyzes attrition rate, turnover trends, high-risk departments and job roles, and key drivers such as overtime and job satisfaction.
  • Employee Demographic Analysis Dashboard

  • Salary & Performance Analysis Dashboard
