Campaign Performance and Customer Composition Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI case study analyzing marketing campaign performance, customer composition, and purchase drivers for a retail food and beverage business.

Power BI DAX Power Query Marketing Analytics Customer Segmentation Business Intelligence

Project Overview

This project helps stakeholders understand which marketing campaigns drive revenue, who the most valuable customers are, and what factors influence purchasing behavior.

  • Evaluate campaign performance by purchases and revenue
  • Identify top-performing products and channels
  • Analyze customer demographics and behavior
  • Determine key purchase drivers

Problem Statement

Despite running multiple marketing campaigns across various products and sales channels, the business lacks a centralized analytical view to clearly evaluate performance and customer behavior. As a result, key business questions remain difficult to answer:

  • Which marketing campaigns generate the highest revenue and purchase volume?
  • Which product categories contribute most significantly to total sales?
  • Who are the most valuable customer segments?
  • What factors most strongly influence campaign acceptance and customer spending behavior?

Without a unified analytical solution, marketing decisions risk being driven by assumptions rather than data-driven insights, limiting campaign optimization and effective resource allocation.

Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

  • Assess marketing campaign effectiveness based on purchases and revenue
  • Identify top-performing products and sales channels
  • Analyze customer demographics and purchasing patterns
  • Determine key drivers influencing campaign acceptance and sales value
  • Present insights in a clear, executive-friendly format for data-driven decision-making

Campaign Performance Analysis Dashboard

Compares campaigns based on purchases, revenue, product mix, and sales channels.

Customer Composition Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes customer demographics such as age, income, education, and household structure.

Purchase Driver Analysis

Identifies factors influencing campaign acceptance and sales value using Key Influencers.

Key Insights

  • Campaign 6 delivered the highest purchases and revenue
  • Wine is the top revenue-generating product
  • High-income, middle-aged customers drive most sales
  • In-store purchases dominate digital channels
